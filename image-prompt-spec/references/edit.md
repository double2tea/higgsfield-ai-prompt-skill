# Edits and references

Skeleton KEEP/REFS blocks are in `../SKILL.md`.

## Edit pass

```text
CHANGE: <the single visible change>
KEEP: identity, pose, crop, background, and every other object
MATCH: original light direction, perspective, and grain
```

If the first edit misses, rewrite CHANGE. Do not stack a second change.

## Roles

| Role | May copy | Must not copy |
|---|---|---|
| IDENTITY | face, hair, body proportions | wardrobe, background, pose |
| STYLE | medium, palette, grain | people, logos, layout |
| PRODUCT | silhouette, logo, materials | scene, model face |
| LIGHT | direction, color, hardness | subject |
| VARIETY | a lineup of distinct people | one face repeated as a crowd |

One identity reference is one person. A crowd needs a VARIETY sheet.

Label images in attachment order. Do not restate a locked face.
